Privacy Policy

Paisa Bharo respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share when registering, completing surveys, and redeeming rewards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Data: Firebase unique user identifier (UID), email address, phone number (if using Phone OTP), display name, and profile photo.
  • Wallet & Transaction Records: Coin balance, lifetime earnings, daily check-in streaks, referral attribution, and payout redemption requests (e.g., UPI ID or gift card delivery email).
  • Technical & Regional Preferences: Selected language, currency, country code, and timezone for accurate reward localization and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • To credit earned coins accurately and process payout redemptions.
  • To match you with geographically relevant surveys, polls, and partner offers.
  • To prevent multi-account abuse, automated bots, and fraudulent postbacks.

3. Third-Party Survey & Offerwall Partners

Pseudonymous Identifiers Only: When launching a survey or offerwall, Paisa Bharo shares only your pseudonymous account identifier (UID / external transaction ID) with partners—never your private password or payout credentials.

We partner with trusted market research and monetization networks, including:

  • TheoremReach: Receives signed session parameters containing your pseudonymous user identifier to deliver surveys and send server-to-server reward and reversal callbacks.
  • Cint, AdGem, and BitLabs: Provide research surveys and sponsored offers tracked via encrypted sub-IDs.
  • Affiliate Networks: Verify cashback conversions when you shop with participating merchants.

We do not sell your personal contact information to third-party data brokers.

4. Local Storage & Progressive Web App Data

Paisa Bharo uses browser storage (such as localStorage and service worker caches) to save your preferred language, currency, and session state so the application loads reliably on mobile and desktop devices.

5. Data Security & Retention

All wallet credits, redemptions, and callback verifications are enforced server-side with HMAC cryptographic signatures and strict Firestore security rules. We retain account and transaction audit records only as long as necessary to operate the service and fulfill anti-fraud or legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to inspect, export, or request permanent deletion of your account and personal data at any time. To request deletion, use the Data & Account Deletion option in the app or email stickadmob@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact stickadmob@gmail.com.
